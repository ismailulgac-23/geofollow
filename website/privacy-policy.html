Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what data Alveron collects, why, and how it is protected.

📅 Effective: March 25, 2026
📄 Last Updated: March 25, 2026

TL;DR — The Short Version

  • We collect your location only when the app is active or in the background (with your permission).
  • Your location is only shared with members of your private group — never publicly.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • You can pause location sharing, delete your data, or delete your account at any time.
  • We use industry-standard encryption to protect all data in transit and at rest.

1. Who We Are

Alveron ("Alveron", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application designed to help families and close-knit groups share their real-time locations privately and securely. This Privacy Policy applies to the Alveron mobile application available on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store), and the associated backend services operated at alveron.app.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@alveron.app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the following information from those providers:

  • Your full name (as registered with Google or Apple)
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photograph (if provided by your provider)
  • A unique user identifier from the provider

We do not receive or store your Google or Apple account passwords.

2.2 Location Data

Location data is the core function of Alveron. We collect:

  • GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude), accurate to approximately 5–15 meters
  • Speed and heading (used for geofence accuracy, not stored permanently)
  • Approximate address (reverse-geocoded from coordinates using the device's geocoding service)
  • Timestamps of each location update
  • Geofence events (entry and exit events for places you have configured)

Location is collected even when the app is in the background, subject to the permissions you grant on your device (iOS: "Always Allow"; Android: "Allow all the time"). You may revoke this permission in your device settings at any time, which will stop location collection immediately.

2.3 Device Information

  • Device battery level (displayed to your group members)
  • Device platform (iOS or Android) and OS version
  • App version number
  • Push notification token (to send geofence and SOS alerts)

2.4 Usage Information

We may collect anonymized, aggregated analytics data such as feature usage frequency, session length, and crash reports to improve app quality. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide location sharing: Your real-time location is transmitted to and displayed for the other members of your private group.
  • To trigger geofence alerts: When you enter or exit a configured safe zone, a notification is sent to group members.
  • To maintain location history: Location snapshots are stored to power the 7-day (free) or 30-day (Premium) history feature.
  • To send push notifications: SOS alerts, geofence events, group invitations, and nudge messages.
  • To authenticate your account: We verify your identity using tokens provided by Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In.
  • To improve the app: Anonymous crash and usage data help us fix bugs and optimize performance.
  • To communicate with you: We may send service-related emails such as account confirmation or security notices.

4. Who Can See Your Location

Your location is exclusively visible to members of the private groups you have joined. Each group is protected by a unique invite code. No one outside your group — including Alveron employees — can see your real-time location unless required by a valid legal order.

At any time you may:

  • Pause location sharing from the app settings (your location becomes hidden from all group members)
  • Leave a group to remove yourself from that group's visibility
  • Delete your account to permanently remove all your data

5. Data Retention

  • Real-time location: Updated every 15 seconds while active; prior values are replaced.
  • Location history snapshots: Retained for 7 days (free tier) or 30 days (Premium). Automatically deleted after the applicable period.
  • Geofence event logs: Retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Crash and analytics logs: Retained for up to 90 days in aggregated, anonymized form.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with the following limited categories of service providers solely to operate Alveron:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Our servers are hosted on industry-leading cloud providers (e.g., AWS, Render). Data is stored in encrypted databases.
  • Apple / Google: Sign-in services only. We receive authentication tokens; we do not share your data back with them.
  • Push notification services: Apple APNs and Google FCM to deliver alerts to your device.
  • Geocoding: Your device's native geocoding service (Apple Maps or Google Maps on-device) converts coordinates to addresses locally where possible.

All third-party providers are contractually required to maintain the confidentiality of your data and may not use it for any other purpose.

7. Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • All data is transmitted over TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS).
  • Location data and account information are stored in encrypted databases.
  • Authentication relies on OAuth 2.0 tokens from Apple and Google — we never handle raw passwords.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We regularly review and test our security practices.

No method of digital transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware, in compliance with applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Alveron is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. Alveron can be used to track the location of minors (e.g., a parent monitoring a child's location) only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who must create and manage the group account.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will delete that data promptly. Contact us at privacy@alveron.app if you believe this has occurred.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Portability: Request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection / Restriction: Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@alveron.app. We will respond within 30 days. You may also delete your account directly from the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account.

10. International Data Transfers

Alveron is operated from Turkey and may process your data on servers located in the United States or European Union. By using the app, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in these jurisdictions. We apply appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required) to protect transferred data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the app and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Alveron after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@alveron.app
  • Support: support@alveron.app
  • Website: alveron.app
