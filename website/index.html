📍 Core Features

Everything your family
needs to stay safe

Powerful location tools designed for families, built with privacy and simplicity in mind.

📍

Real-Time Tracking

See your family members' exact location on a live map, updated every 15 seconds. Works in background without draining battery.

🛡️

Smart Geofences

Create custom safe zones around home, school, work or any place. Get instant push notifications when family members arrive or leave.

🚨

SOS Emergency

One-tap SOS button instantly alerts all group members with your real-time location. Critical for emergencies, works even on poor connections.

⚡

Battery Monitoring

See your family members' battery levels at a glance. Never worry about a loved one going offline unexpectedly.

🕐

Location History

Review up to 30 days of location history. See where your family has been, when, and for how long — with a clear timeline view.

👥

Group Sharing

Create a private group, invite family members with a code, and share locations securely. Only people you invite can see your location.

Up and running in
3 simple steps

1

Download & Sign In

Download Alveron from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in securely with your Google or Apple account — no password needed. Your account is created automatically.

2

Create or Join a Group

Create a private group and share your unique invite code with family members. Or enter an invite code to join an existing group. Locations are only visible within your private group.

3

Add Places & Stay Connected

Add important locations like home, school, and work as safe zones. Set your preferred notification radius and receive instant alerts whenever a family member arrives or leaves.
